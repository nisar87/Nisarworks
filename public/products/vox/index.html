Vox

Say it. Your PC does it.

A voice assistant that runs your Windows 11 PC: say what you want, it does it, and it tells you what it did.

Early releasesv0.4.3Windows 11
Download for WindowsSource on GitHub

Things you can say

  • “Open YouTube in Chrome and search for lofi beats”
  • “Create a folder on the desktop and move everything not opened in 20 days into it”
  • “How much disk space is left?”
  • “Open Notepad and keep writing what I say”
  • “Undo that”

The idea

A computer that listens like a person would.

Say "Hi Vox", or press Ctrl+Alt+Space, and ask in your own words. Vox hears the whole request, does every part of it, and answers briefly.

It asks before deleting, shutting down, reorganising a folder or running PowerShell. Files are moved, never overwritten, and "Undo that" puts them back.

From voice to done

  1. You say it

    Say "Hi Vox", press the hotkey, or type.

  2. Vox picks a route

    Instant, learned, or one request to the AI.

  3. It asks first when it matters

    Deleting, shutting down, reorganising a folder and running PowerShell wait for your yes or no.

  4. It does every step

    Files are moved, never overwritten.

  5. It tells you what it did

    With how long hearing, thinking and doing each took. "Undo that" puts files back.

Why it is fast, and gets faster

Vox tries the cheapest route first, and shows which one each command took.

  1. Instant

    Everyday commands: open a program or a site, search, volume, media, lock, screenshot, undo.

    No AI request

  2. Learned

    Anything the AI has planned for you before, and close variations of it.

    No AI request

  3. AI

    Everything else. The model returns every step at once.

    One request

What stays on your PC, and what leaves it

  • 01

    Stays on your PC

    Your API keys, settings, the undo journal and the commands Vox has learned.

  • 02

    Sent to your AI provider

    The text of commands that need the AI, with the names of your installed programs and standard folders.

  • 03

    Your voice

    With speech recognition set to "On this PC only", audio never leaves the machine. In a live conversation, your voice is streamed to the provider you chose.

  • 04

    Standby

    By default, speech near the PC is sent to your provider to listen for "Hi Vox". A setting keeps that check on the PC. Standby pauses while you are on a call.

Honest limits

  • You bring your own Gemini or OpenAI API key.
  • Microphone capture, the hotkey, spoken replies and standby cannot be tested on build machines, so they are only checked on real PCs.
  • The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows asks you to confirm the first run.
