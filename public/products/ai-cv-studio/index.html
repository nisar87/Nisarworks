The problem

Good experience, hidden behind a tired document.

Most people have a CV somewhere: a PDF from years ago, a Word file, sometimes only a printout. Starting again from a blank template is the part nobody enjoys.

AI CV Studio takes what you already have, including a photo of a paper CV, or asks a few plain questions if you have nothing. You pick a look, adjust it, and download a PDF.

What it does

  • 01

    Starts from your CV

    Upload a PDF, a Word file or a photo of a printed page.

  • 02

    Or from a conversation

    Say what kind of work you do and answer a few questions in plain language.

  • 03

    32 designs in 8 families

    Clean, Classic, Sidebar, Editorial, Timeline, Bold, Portrait and Compact grid. The gallery shows each one with your own CV.

  • 04

    Describe the design you imagine

    Say it in words, such as "two columns, navy, skills on the left", and get three versions of your own CV to choose from. Then change details in words.

  • 05

    Writing help that does not invent

    It improves your wording. It does not add jobs, dates, qualifications or numbers you did not give it.

  • 06

    A check before you download

    It points out missing contact details, weak bullets, date problems and pages that do not fit, and takes you to the exact field.

  • 07

    Readable by hiring software

    Every design has a version made for online applications, and the PDF keeps real text.

  • 08

    PDF and Word

    Download a PDF, or a Word file that opens cleanly in Word and Google Docs.

  • 09

    Help with the job hunt

    A tracker for the jobs you apply to, cover letters with a covering email, a match against the job advert, and interview practice.

Good to know

  • You can see your new CV without signing in. Downloading it needs a free account.
  • Eight of the 32 designs are free, one in each family. The others need the paid Premium plan, and so do designs you make by describing them.
  • The job tracker is free for up to three jobs. Matching your CV to an advert and interview practice are part of Premium.
  • When you describe a design, only figures about your CV, such as how long it is, go to the AI. The text of your CV does not.
  • It works with Latin scripts only for now.
