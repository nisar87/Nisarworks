ClearWrite

A proofreader that stays on your PC.

Checks spelling and grammar as you write in your usual Windows apps, on your own computer, with no account and no internet connection.

The problem

Help with your writing should not need your writing sent away.

Most writing assistants send what you type to a server. That is a lot of trust for a spelling check.

ClearWrite does the checking on your own computer by default. It needs no account, no key and no connection. Help from an AI service is there if you decide to switch it on, and only then.

Two modes, and you choose

The default

Offline only

  • Spelling and grammar are checked on your device
  • Nothing you write is sent anywhere
  • Works with no internet connection
Only if you switch it on

AI-assisted

  • Rephrase, change tone, continue, suggest a reply
  • Uses your own Gemini key or your ChatGPT plan
  • Only the paragraphs that changed are sent, after you pause

What it does

  • 01

    Underlines where you type

    Red for spelling, blue for grammar, green for things it learned from you.

  • 02

    Never changes text by itself

    You accept each fix. A change that alters a number, a name or a "not" is flagged before it is applied.

  • 03

    Learns your habits

    Accept a fix twice and it is offered again. Ignore a suggestion twice and it stays ignored. All of it is stored on your PC.

  • 04

    Knows where not to look

    Password fields, address bars and apps you exclude are left alone.

  • 05

    One shortcut

    Select text anywhere and press Ctrl+Shift+Space.

  • 06

    Your own dictionary

    Add names and unusual words so they are not flagged.

Honest limits

  • It is a beta. Expect changes between versions.
  • Checking is for English.
  • It does not read the conversation around your text. To get a suggested reply, you select the message yourself.
