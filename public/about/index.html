About

Hi, I'm Nisar. Thanks for stopping by.

Most of what I build starts with a small annoyance: something that's missing, a step that's slower than it should be, a job I keep doing by hand.

Sometimes I need the fix myself. Sometimes the idea is just too interesting to leave alone. When it turns out useful, I put it here, free for anyone.

Nisarworks is the home for all of it. It is not a company and it is not selling anything. It is where my software lives.

How it usually goes

  1. 01

    Notice

    Something small gets in the way. Then it does it again.

  2. 02

    Wonder

    Is there a simple tool for this? If not, could I make one?

  3. 03

    Build

    My favourite part. Lots of trying, breaking things and learning.

  4. 04

    Share

    If it helped me, it might help you. So it goes up here.

  5. 05

    Repeat

    "It works" is rarely the end. There is usually a better way.

Why I share them

Not every project needs a business plan. Some just need a good question and a free evening.

Sometimes a problem is interesting, a solution seems possible, and building it is reward enough. Each product page says plainly what the software can and can't do yet.

Come say hi.

Use anything you find here. If something breaks or could work better, open an issue on its GitHub page. And if you have an idea for something that should exist, I'd like to hear it.
